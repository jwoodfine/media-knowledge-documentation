This media kit collects the material a journalist, analyst, or partner needs to write about PointSav accurately: how the company describes itself, which marks are trademarked and on what terms they may be used, where to direct press inquiries, and the licence that covers this wiki's content.

## About PointSav Digital Systems

PointSav Digital Systems builds operating systems and services for regulated businesses
that need to own their data, their AI, and their record-keeping outright. The platform
runs on customer hardware and produces continuous-disclosure-grade audit trails.

PointSav is currently a trade name of Woodfine Capital Projects Inc., planned to become a wholly-owned Woodfine subsidiary upon incorporation.

## Brand and Wordmarks

**PointSav Digital Systems™** and the PointSav wordmark are trademarks of
Woodfine Capital Projects Inc., used in Canada, the United States, Latin America,
and Europe.

Use of the PointSav wordmark in press and media requires written permission from
Woodfine Capital Projects Inc. For inquiries, contact
[open.source@pointsav.com](mailto:open.source@pointsav.com).

## Press Inquiries

Media and press inquiries should be directed to Woodfine Capital Projects Inc.

- **General contact:** [open.source@pointsav.com](mailto:open.source@pointsav.com)
